Final Data Availability statement

The public reproducibility materials supporting the principal numerical claims of this study are archived on Zenodo under DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22547701 and mirrored in the GitHub repository https://github.com/Alexandregiacomellileal/active-surface-potential-grounding. The archive includes frozen protocol records, deterministic design-of-experiments provenance, MATLAB implementations of the core inverse/acquisition procedures, post-held-out measurement-noise stress-test materials, and derived summary tables used to verify the principal reported results. The 84-case held-out evaluation was completed before public release of the archive and no method retuning was performed after those outcomes were disclosed. The complete corpus of all 84 COMSOL-generated 956-point surface fields is not included in the compact public archive and may be deposited separately if required by the journal or editor.
