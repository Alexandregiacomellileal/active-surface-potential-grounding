Proposed Data Availability statement (replace DOI placeholder after Zenodo publication)

The source code, frozen protocol records, deterministic design-of-experiments provenance,
derived result tables required to reproduce the reported figures and numerical summaries,
and the post-held-out measurement-noise stress-test materials are available in a public
reproducibility archive at Zenodo (DOI: DOI_TO_BE_ASSIGNED) and mirrored at
https://github.com/Alexandregiacomellileal/active-surface-potential-grounding.
The 84-case held-out evaluation was completed before publication of the archive and was
not retuned after its outcomes were disclosed. Full COMSOL-generated surface-field files
may be archived as a separate dataset if journal/editorial requirements call for the
complete model-output corpus.
